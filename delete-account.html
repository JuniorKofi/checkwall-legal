Delete your Check Wall account and data

Check Wall by Junior Kofi Studios · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Privacy PolicyTerms of ServiceDelete account

Option 1 – Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open Check Wall.
  2. Tap the Profile tab at the bottom.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with Delete. You may be asked to sign in again first.

Option 2 – Ask us by email

If you no longer have the app, email owarejuniorgh@gmail.com with the subject “Check Wall account deletion” from the email address linked to your account (or include your in-game username). We delete the account and data within 30 days and confirm by email. You can use the same address to ask us to delete only some of your data (for example your leaderboard entry) while keeping your account.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication) and its linked email / Google / Apple sign-in.
  • Your public profile: username, online rating, wins and losses, campaign progress and leaderboard entry.
  • Your private record of your Remove Ads subscription status.

What is kept

  • Progress and settings stored only on your device stay there until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Online match rooms may keep the moves of past games together with the random player ID and display name used in that match; email us to have these removed too.
  • Google Play keeps its own purchase records as required by law. Deleting your account does not cancel a Remove Ads subscription – cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
  • Advertising data held by Google AdMob is governed by Google's Privacy Policy; you can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
